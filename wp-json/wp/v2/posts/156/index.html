Demonstrators gathered outside several hotels near St. Cloud, expressing opposition to recent actions by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) in the area. The protest took place amid an uptick in ICE operations, which organizers say has affected local immigrant communities.<\/p>\n\n

Demonstration Details<\/h2>\n\n

On Thursday, a group of protesters assembled outside hotels in the St. Cloud region, voicing concerns over the presence of ICE agents and their impact on residents. Participants held signs and chanted messages calling for changes to current immigration enforcement practices.<\/p>\n\n

Community Response to ICE Operations<\/h2>\n\n

Organizers of the demonstration stated that the increased ICE activity has led to heightened anxiety among immigrants living in the area. According to protest leaders, families have reported feeling unsafe and uncertain about their future due to the recent enforcement actions.<\/p>\n\n

Statements from Protest Organizers<\/h2>\n\n

One organizer commented, \u201cWe are here to support our neighbors and to demand an end to these raids that are tearing families apart.\u201d The group urged local officials to take steps to protect community members from what they described as aggressive enforcement tactics.<\/p>\n\n

Hotel Involvement<\/h2>\n\n

Protesters claimed that some hotels in the vicinity have been used by ICE agents during their operations. Demonstrators called on hotel management to reconsider allowing federal agents to stay on their premises, citing concerns about the safety and well-being of local residents.<\/p>\n\n

Ongoing Concerns<\/h2>\n\n
