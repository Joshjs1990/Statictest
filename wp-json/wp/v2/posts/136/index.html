Protesters assembled outside a Minneapolis hotel on Friday night, where they believed federal agents were staying after an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officer shot and killed a man earlier that day.<\/p>\n\n

The demonstration took place at the Hyatt Regency hotel in downtown Minneapolis. Organizers and participants stated that they believed ICE agents involved in the shooting were present at the hotel. The protest began in the evening and continued into the night, with participants chanting and holding signs in opposition to ICE operations.<\/p>\n\n

Incident Leading to the Protest<\/h2>\n\n

The protest was prompted by an incident earlier on Friday, when an ICE officer fatally shot a man during an enforcement action in Minneapolis. According to ICE, the shooting occurred as officers attempted to arrest the individual, who was wanted on a federal warrant. ICE stated that the man produced a firearm during the encounter, leading to the officer firing his weapon.<\/p>\n\n

The identity of the man who was killed has not been released. The incident is currently under investigation by local authorities and federal officials. ICE reported that no officers were injured during the incident.<\/p>\n\n

Community Response<\/h2>\n\n

Following the shooting, community members and activists quickly organized a protest at the Hyatt Regency. Demonstrators called for accountability and transparency regarding the actions of ICE officers. Some participants expressed concerns about the presence of federal agents in the city and the impact of ICE enforcement on local communities.<\/p>\n\n

Organizers used social media to coordinate the demonstration, urging supporters to gather at the hotel. The protest remained peaceful, with no reports of arrests or injuries as of late Friday night.<\/p>\n\n

Ongoing Investigation<\/h2>\n\n

Authorities have stated that the shooting is under review. The Minneapolis Police Department and federal officials are conducting separate investigations into the circumstances surrounding the incident. ICE has indicated that it is cooperating fully with the inquiries.<\/p>\n\n
