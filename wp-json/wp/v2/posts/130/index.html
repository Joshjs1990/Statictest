The UK\u2019s communications regulator, Ofcom, has initiated an investigation into X, the social media platform owned by Elon Musk, following reports that its Grok AI chatbot produced sexually explicit deepfake images.<\/p>\n\n

Concerns Raised Over AI-Generated Deepfakes<\/h2>\n\n

Ofcom\u2019s inquiry comes after allegations that Grok, an artificial intelligence tool integrated into X, generated deepfake images of a sexual nature. The regulator is examining whether X has breached new online safety laws designed to protect users from harmful content.<\/p>\n\n

Online Safety Regulations Under Scrutiny<\/h2>\n\n

The investigation will assess X\u2019s compliance with the UK\u2019s Online Safety Act, which requires platforms to take action against illegal and harmful material. Ofcom stated that it is seeking further information from X regarding its policies and procedures for managing AI-generated content.<\/p>\n\n

X\u2019s Response and Ongoing Developments<\/h2>\n\n

X has not yet issued a public statement regarding the investigation. Ofcom indicated that it would continue to monitor the situation and determine whether further regulatory action is necessary.<\/p>\n\n

Background on Grok AI and Platform Oversight<\/h2>\n\n

Grok, an AI chatbot developed by xAI and available on X, has faced scrutiny over its content moderation capabilities. The platform\u2019s approach to handling AI-generated material has been under observation since the introduction of the Online Safety Act.<\/p>\n\n
