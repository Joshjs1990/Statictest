Two Dallas-based companies have purchased an office building in Uptown Dallas, announcing intentions to transform the site with a new hotel project valued at $50 million.<\/p>\n\n

Acquisition of Maple Avenue Property<\/h2>\n\n

Urban Genesis and Mintwood Real Estate have acquired the office building located at 2626 Maple Avenue. The property, which encompasses approximately 50,000 square feet, was previously owned by a local investment group. The transaction was finalized earlier this month, according to representatives from the purchasing firms.<\/p>\n\n

Hotel Project Details<\/h2>\n\n

The new owners plan to demolish the existing structure and construct a 14-story hotel on the site. The proposed development is expected to feature around 200 rooms, with amenities including a rooftop bar, restaurant, and event space. Construction is anticipated to begin in early 2025, pending necessary city approvals.<\/p>\n\n

Statements from Developers<\/h2>\n\n

\u201cWe believe this location is ideal for a boutique hotel that will serve both business and leisure travelers,\u201d said Urban Genesis co-founder Matt Edwards. \u201cUptown Dallas continues to be a vibrant destination, and we look forward to contributing to its growth.\u201d<\/p>\n\n

Mintwood Real Estate principal John Harris added, \u201cOur vision is to create a unique hospitality experience that complements the surrounding neighborhood and offers new options for visitors and residents alike.\u201d<\/p>\n\n

Location and Market Context<\/h2>\n\n

The property is situated near the intersection of Maple Avenue and Mahon Street, within close proximity to several major office towers, restaurants, and retail establishments. Uptown Dallas has seen increased investment in recent years, with multiple hotel and mixed-use projects underway.<\/p>\n\n

Project Timeline and Investment<\/h2>\n\n

The developers estimate the total investment in the hotel project will be approximately $50 million. The timeline for completion is projected for late 2026, subject to permitting and construction schedules.<\/p>\n\n","protected":false},"excerpt":{"rendered":"
