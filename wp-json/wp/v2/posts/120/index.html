A recent study has found that numerous families in the metro Atlanta region are living in extended stay hotels for prolonged periods, with many unable to secure permanent housing.<\/p>\n\n

Report Highlights Housing Challenges<\/h2>\n\n

The study, conducted by the Atlanta Regional Collaborative for Health Improvement (ARCHI), indicates that thousands of families are currently residing in extended stay hotels across the area. According to the findings, these families often remain in such accommodations for months or even years, citing barriers including high rental costs, insufficient affordable housing, and difficulties meeting rental application requirements.<\/p>\n\n

Families Describe Feeling ‘Trapped’<\/h2>\n\n

Researchers interviewed several families who described feeling “trapped” in extended stay hotels due to financial and logistical obstacles. One parent shared, “We want to move into an apartment, but the deposits and credit checks make it impossible.” The study notes that many families spend a significant portion of their income on hotel fees, which can exceed the cost of traditional rent.<\/p>\n\n

Limited Housing Options<\/h2>\n\n

The report details that metro Atlanta’s shortage of affordable housing has contributed to the rise in families turning to extended stay hotels as a last resort. The study found that some families have lived in these hotels for over a year, with limited access to kitchen facilities and other amenities typically available in permanent housing.<\/p>\n\n

Community Organizations Respond<\/h2>\n\n

Local organizations are working to address the issue by providing resources and support to families living in extended stay hotels. ARCHI representatives stated that increased collaboration between government agencies, nonprofits, and housing providers is needed to create more affordable housing opportunities and assist families in transitioning to stable homes.<\/p>\n\n
