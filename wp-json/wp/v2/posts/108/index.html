Iranian authorities stated on Tuesday that they have brought an end to the recent wave of protests that erupted across the country, following reports from human rights organizations indicating that hundreds of demonstrators were killed during the unrest.<\/p>\n\n

Government Response to Demonstrations<\/h2>\n\n

The Iranian government reported that security forces have restored order after days of widespread protests. Officials described the demonstrations as riots, attributing them to foreign interference and criminal elements. According to state television, authorities have detained numerous individuals allegedly involved in the disturbances.<\/p>\n\n

Casualty Figures and Human Rights Concerns<\/h2>\n\n

Amnesty International and other human rights groups have estimated that at least 208 people died during the protests, which began on November 15 following a sudden increase in fuel prices. Some reports suggest the death toll could be higher, with unconfirmed figures reaching up to 400. The Iranian government has not released official casualty numbers.<\/p>\n\n

Internet Access and Communication Restrictions<\/h2>\n\n

During the height of the protests, Iranian officials imposed a near-total shutdown of internet access across the country, severely limiting the flow of information. Authorities have since begun restoring connectivity, though access remains restricted in certain regions.<\/p>\n\n

International Reactions<\/h2>\n\n

The United States and several European governments have condemned the Iranian authorities’ response to the protests. U.S. Secretary of State Mike Pompeo called on Iran to restore internet access and respect the rights of its citizens. The European Union also expressed concern over the reported use of excessive force against demonstrators.<\/p>\n\n

Background of the Protests<\/h2>\n\n

The unrest began after the government announced a significant increase in fuel prices, sparking demonstrations in cities and towns nationwide. Protesters voiced frustration over economic hardship and government policies. The protests quickly escalated, with reports of violence and property damage in some areas.<\/p>\n\n

Ongoing Developments<\/h2>\n\n

While Iranian officials have declared the protests over, human rights groups continue to call for accountability and transparency regarding the use of force and the number of casualties. The situation remains under close observation by international organizations and foreign governments.<\/p>\n\n
