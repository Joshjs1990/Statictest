The United Nations Secretary-General has stated he is \u201cshocked\u201d by recent reports indicating the use of excessive force by security personnel against demonstrators in Iran.<\/p>\n\n

According to a spokesperson for the UN chief, Ant\u00f3nio Guterres, the Secretary-General is following the situation in Iran closely and is alarmed by accounts of violence against people participating in protests. The spokesperson emphasized that the Secretary-General urges Iranian authorities to respect the rights to freedom of expression, peaceful assembly, and association.<\/p>\n\n

The statement from the United Nations comes amid ongoing demonstrations in Iran, which have reportedly been met with a forceful response from security forces. The UN has called for all incidents involving excessive force to be thoroughly investigated and for those responsible to be held accountable.<\/p>\n\n

The Secretary-General reiterated the importance of upholding human rights and fundamental freedoms, particularly in the context of peaceful protest.
