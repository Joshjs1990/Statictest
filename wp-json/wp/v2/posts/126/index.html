A hotel located in Frisco has been included in a list of the best new hotels in the United States.<\/p>\n\n

The Omni PGA Frisco Resort was named one of the top new hotels in the country by USA Today\u2019s 10Best Readers\u2019 Choice Awards. The resort, which opened in May 2023, is situated on the PGA Frisco campus and features 500 guest rooms, 10 restaurants and bars, two championship golf courses, and a spa.<\/p>\n\n

According to the 10Best website, the list highlights hotels that have opened within the past two years and have quickly become favorites among travelers. The Omni PGA Frisco Resort was ranked No. 7 on the list.<\/p>\n\n

\u201cWe are honored to be recognized by USA Today\u2019s 10Best as one of the best new hotels in the country,\u201d said Jeff Smith, managing director of Omni PGA Frisco Resort, in a statement. \u201cOur team is dedicated to providing exceptional experiences for our guests, and this recognition is a testament to their hard work and commitment.\u201d<\/p>\n\n
