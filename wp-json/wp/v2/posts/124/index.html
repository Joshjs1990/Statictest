Authorities in Minneapolis detained 30 people overnight after protests intensified at the Hilton Canopy Hotel, where U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents were reportedly present. Demonstrators gathered at the hotel in response to reports of ICE activity, leading to a confrontation with law enforcement.<\/p>\n\n

Protests Intensify at Downtown Hotel<\/h2>\n\n

The demonstration began on Monday evening outside the Hilton Canopy Hotel in downtown Minneapolis. Protesters assembled following reports that ICE agents were operating from the hotel. The situation escalated as the night progressed, resulting in a significant police response.<\/p>\n\n

Police Response and Arrests<\/h2>\n\n

According to officials, law enforcement intervened after protesters allegedly blocked entrances and disrupted hotel operations. Minneapolis police stated that 30 individuals were taken into custody during the incident. Authorities said the arrests were made for alleged trespassing and failure to comply with orders to disperse.<\/p>\n\n

ICE Presence and Community Reaction<\/h2>\n\n

ICE representatives confirmed that agents were present at the hotel as part of ongoing operations but did not provide further details regarding the nature of their activities. Community members and advocacy groups expressed concern about the use of local hotels for federal immigration enforcement efforts, prompting the demonstration.<\/p>\n\n

Statements from Officials<\/h2>\n\n

Minneapolis city officials acknowledged the community\u2019s concerns and stated that they are monitoring the situation. A spokesperson for the Hilton Canopy Hotel said the property is cooperating with authorities and prioritizing the safety of guests and staff.<\/p>\n\n

Ongoing Developments<\/h2>\n\n

The incident remains under investigation, and city officials indicated that further information will be released as it becomes available. Advocacy groups have called for increased transparency regarding ICE operations in the area.<\/p>\n\n
